# Physics Review: Simultaneous Three-Qubit Model

15 October 2026. Separate internal AI-assisted interpretation review.
No independent physicist certification or experimental validation is claimed.

## Verdict

Keep as a conditional operational example and close this bounded gate. It
removes the earlier example's separately timed memory-copy operation from
the modeled interaction interval. It does NOT remove the supplied subsystem
decomposition, apparatus architecture, reference preparation, timing, fresh
input replacement, or final readout. It is not an ICC selector candidate
established by these results.

## What Is Physically Consistent

- Resonant exchange is a standard finite interaction class. A fixed
  conditional memory rotation is a coherent apparatus model with both
  couplings present during the whole exposure.
- M's logical states are assigned the same conserved charge. They can be
  interpreted as an encoded internal apparatus degree of freedom, not a
  new spatial dimension or an unknown cosmological sector. An actual
  implementation of this idealization is not supplied.
- The initial coherent detector is a finite, explicitly supplied asymmetry
  resource. The population pointers respect the modeled Q symmetry; the
  induced transverse system measurements are unsharp.
- The memory has an operationally distinguishable partial preparation-label
  record. Its allowed population readout does worse than an unrestricted
  optimal measurement; the two numbers are not conflated.

## Strongest Objections and Their Consequences

1. **Record formation does not explain the detector change here.** A maximally
   mixed memory has no local label record but yields identical D statistics,
   even through subsequent unread uses. For this pointer the model reduces
   to conserved-X conditional-detuning sectors. Joint correlations remain;
   the control is not a theorem that records are universally irrelevant.
2. **The memory coupling and split are supplied.** A Hamiltonian written on
   S tensor D tensor M cannot establish why nature selects that decomposition.
   The physical justification is apparatus-level plausibility, not a
   derivation from ICC or gravity. Do not call it a discovered natural law.
3. **Priming is not a completed first measurement.** The before effect is
   tested in separate fresh-apparatus trials. The priming exposure has no
   readout; tracing out the old S does not secretly measure D. A fresh S is
   inserted externally for the second exposure. Fully autonomous repeated
   operation would require input transport and timing beyond this model.
4. **Q conservation is not energy-free operation.** Both Q and total-H energy
   are conserved during each unitary exposure. But population readout does
   not commute with the full interacting Hamiltonian. Readout, preparation,
   replacement, and work costs have not been modeled as a closed apparatus.
5. **The memory is not a permanent classical record.** Exchange changes D
   during recording, so the interaction is not QND for D under the full H.
   A closed finite system permits recurrences and reversal; no stable,
   redundant, irreversible record or macroscopic outcome has been derived.
6. **This is not a new algebra or a reset.** The induced POVM changes relative
   to a fixed laboratory access specification. No global factorization
   changes, no entropy contrast is selected, and no cosmological observation
   follows. The declared clock and initial reference remain essential.

## Research Boundary

No fourth-qubit extension, new ontology, or additional cosmological postulate
is justified by this gate alone. Keep the broader ICC question narrow:
which independently motivated physical inputs determine measurement access?
The distinction between successive aeons and observer-relative descriptions
is not decided by the present finite model. Multiple possible mathematical
descriptions do not require infinitely many actual observers, and observer
access does not mean conscious choice of physical reality.

The literature scope is stated in the README: exchange transport, finite
reference resources, and conservation-constrained measurements are prior
physics. No novelty or corroboration of ICC is claimed. Release artifacts
remain unchanged.
