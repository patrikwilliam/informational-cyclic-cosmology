# Release Notes: v0.1.7

Release date: 10 October 2026.

This update adds a bounded arithmetic reconstruction and its limitations. It
does not introduce a physical selector, change the core postulates, or reopen
the earlier lattice or finite-time searches.

## Changes

1. Appendix B specifies the exact logarithmic, nondegenerate Hamiltonian and
   proves conditional diagonal-unitary equivalence of compatible multiplication
   isometries. A supplied coherent reference selects one representative.
2. The stationary-state argument shows equal restricted entropy at each fixed
   prime across these embeddings. Free reference evolution leaves the algebra
   unchanged. This is not a universal no-go theorem for ICC or arithmetic models.
3. A fixed-reference infinite example has vanishing reference-state trace
   distance but maximal Kadison-Kastler distance. It limits unrestricted
   full-norm reconstruction, not all operational stability criteria.
4. The supplement supplies the derivation, scoped literature review,
   reproducible diagnostics, recorded results, and separate internal
   mathematical and physical reviews. The review clarifies phase conjugation,
   unbounded-H stationarity, the exact distance convention, and access costs.
5. The abstract, summary, README, citation metadata, open questions, source
   audit, AI-assistance disclosure, PDF and source package are aligned.
6. Appendix B.4 adds a fixed-window effect-set comparison. The old high-index
   flip becomes invisible, but a common reference energy ceiling does not
   guarantee uniform continuity. A positive accessible reference-amplitude
   floor yields an explicit local conditioning bound.
7. The package includes scoped Lean sources, pinned dependency metadata and
   an audit of 16 declarations, plus two rejected false-proof controls. The
   reconstruction, infinite reference sums, trace-distance formulas, and
   assembled operational theorem remain analytic. No whole-paper formal
   certification is claimed. The numerical runner now has 24 diagnostic groups.
8. The pre-publication clarification of 11 October explicitly closes the
   reference-phase-only route as an ICC transition candidate under the stated
   arithmetic assumptions. It retains the reconstruction and stability results
   and leaves the broader selector question open. This is a research-status
   consequence of the existing obstruction, not a new theorem or calculation.

## Boundaries

- The arithmetic inputs are assumed, not derived from cosmology.
- A polar decomposition is not a demonstrated implementable operation.
- External coherent test preparation and calibration do not enlarge the
  candidate measurement sets. The hard cutoff concerns test states; the
  mean-energy budget concerns references. No bounded implementation cost,
  mixed-noise, spectral-error or continuum-limit guarantee follows.
- Fixed-window local continuity and failure of uniform continuity are distinct.
  The conditional bound does not repair stationary entropy equality or the
  absence of an algebra transition under free reference evolution.
- Type-I prime-occupation factors are not spatially local continuum QFT algebras.
- Bost-Connes reconstruction starts from a rich algebra in its specified class;
  it does not reconstruct that class from an arbitrary spectrum.
- No new OpenAI theorem, Riemann-hypothesis result or prime-number postulate is
  used. The paused family-290/282 review is not part of this update.
- All reviews are internal and AI-assisted; no independent expert approval or
  complete re-audit of older work is claimed.
- Author, ORCID, CC BY 4.0 license and all-versions DOI are unchanged. No new
  version-specific DOI is invented, and no upload is performed by preparation.
