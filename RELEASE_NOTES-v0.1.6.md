# Release Notes: v0.1.6

Release date: 9 October 2026.

This is a bounded mathematical-clarification and reproducibility update, not
a new physical mechanism or a change in the core cosmological postulates.

## Changes

1. Section 5.4 adds a direct fixed-dimension bound on restricted von Neumann
   entropy in terms of Kadison-Kastler distance between equal-size finite
   matrix factors. The full supplement gives the proof, endpoint error budget,
   supplied-unitary bound and growing-dimension counterexample. No novelty
   or extension to cosmological entropy is claimed.
2. Section 6 adds conditional prior art from Zanardi's finite observable-algebra
   transport framework. An explicit horizontal path supports a fixed-state
   entropy comparison, but its path and stopping point are prescribed.
   Co-transporting state and algebra removes the contrast. This is not a
   physical selector or a new no-go theorem for ICC.
3. Section 2.2 clarifies that inaccessible massive degrees of freedom do not
   automatically establish conformal invariance: their dynamical and
   gravitational effects must still be addressed.
4. Section 5.1 requires consistent trace/renormalization calibration and horizon
   accounting for cross-algebra gravitational entropy comparisons.
5. The supplement, citation audit, open questions and AI-assistance disclosure
   now cover the bounded additions. Two scripts check 13 finite diagnostic
   groups; an assertion-enabled runner preserves the recorded output.
6. Version/date metadata, README, formatted PDF and source archive are aligned.
   The author, ORCID, CC BY 4.0 license and all-versions DOI are unchanged.
   No unassigned version-specific DOI is inserted.

## Unchanged Boundaries

- No physical algebra-selection rule, cosmological entropy functional,
  transition dynamics, repeatable aeonic cycle or prediction is derived.
- Earlier unrestricted-selector, EC/CAR and finite-time results are unchanged.
  Their closed numerical searches are not restarted.
- The new bound is not a stability theorem for unspecified POVMs, type-III
  algebras or gravitational entropy. Fixed finite factor dimension is essential.
- The OpenAI catalogue's reported universal stability theorem and Lean scope
  are documented as provenance in the supplement, not adopted as an ICC axiom
  or independently compiled/certified here.
- All review and verification remains internal and AI-assisted, not expert
  peer review or external validation of the cosmological proposal.
