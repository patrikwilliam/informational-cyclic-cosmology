# v0.1.8 Physics Review

16 October 2026. Separate internal AI-assisted interpretation pass, not
independent expert validation or a review of all historical cosmology claims.

## Verdict

The increment supports a limited operational result under supplied resources:
after unread priming, accessible phase-reference quality can become too weak
to realize a previously implemented effect, even when every symmetric joint
measurement on the retained apparatus is granted. It does not derive the
physical restriction that makes this conclusion applicable.

## Boundaries Retained

- The tensor factors, symmetry, charge-neutral memory, Hamiltonian, clock,
  fresh-input supply and old-input inaccessibility are specified in advance.
  They have not emerged from ICC. The memory is not an extra spatial dimension.
- The processing benchmark is the same before and after, broader than the
  actual hardware. Its optimum supplies a ceiling, not evidence that every
  optimal measurement is physically implemented by that fixed Hamiltonian.
- Old-input preparations have no accessible label or purification. No extra
  asymmetric references, known coherent training inputs, copies or discarded
  failures are smuggled into the comparison. Charge conservation alone does
  not justify excluding such resources from a real laboratory.
- Retaining the old input with full symmetric control makes the resource
  transformation reversible and the complete capability sets equal. The
  local loss is not global information destruction or an inevitable arrow.
- A maximally mixed memory yields the same original detector statistics and
  optimal phase-task score without the local preparation-label record. This
  undermines a record-driven interpretation of this example, not every possible
  role of records. Equal task scores do not imply equal full capability sets.
- The two reduced effect sets span the same `M_2` algebra. Their quantitative
  distinction supplies neither algebra selection nor a cosmological entropy
  functional, reset, repeatability or observable cosmological prediction.
- Established finite-reference/asymmetry and discrimination literature is
  attributed as prior art. No outside team's work is presented as endorsement
  of ICC or proof of the example's cosmological relevance.

## Strategy

Appendix C is an operational clarification, not a promoted transition
mechanism. Core postulates and the intended aeonic interpretation remain
unchanged. There is no justification here for another large finite search;
the open issue is independently motivated access and physical algebra choice.
The partial Lean checks strengthen the bounded mathematics, not the empirical
credibility of the cosmological hypothesis.

## Post-Integration Re-Review

16 October 2026, after preparation of the v0.1.8 PDF. This is a fresh internal
physics pass, separate from the mathematical checks recorded alongside it.
It covers Appendix C and the corresponding abstract, conclusion, release-note
and supplement claims, not a new audit of all earlier versions.

**No blocking physical inconsistency or new overclaim was found in this
increment. No manuscript correction is required by this pass.**

The strongest attempted objections were:

1. **Hidden change of controls.** The full symmetric processing class is fixed
   on both sides. Its after ceiling excludes an effect already implemented by
   the original hardware, so the result is stronger than a deteriorating chosen
   readout. This does not mean the fixed Hamiltonian implements the whole class.
2. **Access restriction doing the physical work.** It is: the inaccessible old
   input turns a reversible unitary into a reduced resource channel. Neither
   charge conservation nor the calculation derives this access restriction.
   The manuscript explicitly supplies it, along with input replacement and
   timing. Thus the example is not a closed autonomous cosmological evolution.
3. **A hidden extra reference.** Initial detector coherence is the supplied
   resource. The phase-defined test ensemble is calibrated relative to it;
   the preparator, old preparation label and purification are inaccessible.
   Repeated copies or a refreshed asymmetric reference would change the task.
4. **Memory as a selector or a necessary cause.** Neutral logical memory is
   consistent with the declared symmetry but is an idealized apparatus choice.
   The mixed-memory control retains the phase-task result without a readable
   local preparation-label record. A record-driven mechanism is not established.
5. **Irreversible global loss or algebra change.** Retaining the old input and
   granting full symmetric control restores equality. Both reduced effect sets
   still span the same qubit algebra. Neither global information destruction
   nor a selected new algebra follows.

The source roles were rechecked against
[Marvian and Spekkens](https://arxiv.org/abs/1212.3378) for measurement simulation
under symmetry and [Bartlett et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0602069) for
finite-reference degradation. These are appropriate prior-art connections,
not external confirmation of the particular Hamiltonian or ICC. No novelty
search or experimental validation was performed.

The release remains defensible as an explicitly conditional operational
clarification. It does not advance the physical selector from an open problem
to a derived mechanism. The manuscript and PDF were left unchanged.
