Date: 1 October 2026

Scope: incorporation of the completed finite-time selector, measurement-entropy
and measurement-access studies into the release. This is not a new full review

claimed.

## Mathematical Integration Review

The finite-time normalization, declared Hamiltonian and horizon, normal-slice
certificate, local/global distinction, explicit competitor, and conditional
recovery bounds were checked against the completed derivations. The fixed
Bell-state measurement example is distinct from the singlet ground-state
selector example. Global, marginal, Choi-purity and observational quantities
are not identified with one another.

The POVM probabilities, global trace volumes, noise/sharpness dependence,
postprocessing and all-input local implementation were checked against the
supplement. A wording error was corrected: the *branch effects*, not the
Kraus operators themselves, sum to the randomized POVM effects, including the
axis probability 1/3.

After correction, no remaining mathematical transcription or integration
blocker was found in the added material. This is not a claim that internal
review excludes all mathematical errors.

## Physical Integration Review

The text distinguishes a strict local minimum from global preference and
physical selection. It reports the product competitor's better cost and
recovery in the declared task, without inferring relaxation dynamics, trapping,
a privileged clock, or a cosmological transition.

Matching outcome statistics is distinguished from implementing coherent
subsystem control or the ideal parity instrument. The abstract's A-only
obstruction was qualified to apply to arbitrary inputs at nonzero sharpness;
known fixed-state statistics and the zero-sharpness limit are not covered by
that obstruction. Access to both original factors remains an extra assumption.

After correction, no remaining physical overclaim or integration blocker was
found in the additions. A cosmological selector, effective entropy, transition,
thermodynamic arrow, repeatability and predictions remain unprovided. These
finite examples neither establish nor exclude ICC in general.

## Reproducibility Checks

The [one-command runner](../research/v015/run_checks.py) passed all six checks
with Python 3.12.14 and NumPy 2.3.5. A separate internal rerun used Python
3.12.13 and NumPy 2.3.5. The runner regenerates the exact certificate before
the dependent operational check and compares the exact coefficient dictionaries
and normalization metadata with the bundled record.

The six calculation scripts and five JSON records are unchanged copies of the
completed working artifacts. The public supplement supplies the analytic
arguments, inputs and limitations without requiring internal working notes.
Temporary rerun outputs do not overwrite the historical records. Assertion
optimization is rejected; the package requires NumPy in the invoking Python
interpreter.

Finite numerical grids, quadrature comparisons and finite differences are
checks within their stated domain, not continuum proofs or arbitrary-noise
robustness claims. The exact finite certificate and analytic all-input access
identities have different evidential roles. Shared helper/coefficient
dependencies and remaining test limitations are listed in the
[reproduction guide](../research/v015/README.md).
