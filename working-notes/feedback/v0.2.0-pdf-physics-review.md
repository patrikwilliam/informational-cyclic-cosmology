## Severity-Ranked Findings (Resolved; Original Text Retained)

### 1. [P2] An approximate implementation needs an operational success criterion, not just proximity to the ideal instrument

**Status: Resolved on 19 October 2026.** Verified in D.5, current PDF page 41, SHA256 `abfde3a47757ed01c4cad8df46e119b68627c209f0dacdd2df4033f27d1de453`. The requested error-class and operational-margin gate, and the caveat about exact scalar intersection, are now explicit. Original finding follows.

**Location:** D.3, pages 39-40; D.5's next-task statement, page 41; D.6's failure model, page 41.

The proposed next task is a resource-accounted derivation or approximation of the labelled instrument. However, approximating its outcome statistics does not by itself preserve the exact claim `S_A intersect S_B = C I`. Exact operator spans can gain directions under arbitrarily small perturbations. The finite-energy witness paragraph correctly disclaims a robustness margin, and the scalar absorbing-failure model covers a particularly restricted error class. The missing step is therefore the acceptance criterion for the proposed approximation, not a false robustness theorem already asserted by the PDF.

An explicit check illustrates the issue while retaining joint compatibility and the same count probabilities. Let `E` be the ideal 21-outcome archive, choose a bounded non-scalar Hermitian field operator `K` with `||K|| <= 1` outside the original five-dimensional span, and write

```text
p0 = exp(-gamma T)
p1 = gamma T exp(-gamma T)
p2 = 1 - p0 - p1 = q2
q1 = p1 + p2
alpha = 1/16

Q0    = p0 I
Q1j   = p1 [I/4 + alpha v_jz K]
Q2jk  = p2 [I/16 + alpha (v_jz + v_kz) K/4]
```

These are positive and sum to identity: `|v_jz| = 1/sqrt(3)`, and the tetrahedral coordinates sum to zero. They also have exactly the original no-tick, one-tick and two-tick probabilities. Their marginals are

```text
A^Q_j = q1 [I/4 + alpha v_jz K]
B^Q_k = q2 [I/4 + alpha v_kz K].
```

For any `0 < epsilon < 1`, the convex mixture `E^epsilon = (1-epsilon)E + epsilon Q` is another compatible archive with each effect at operator-norm distance at most `epsilon` from the ideal effect. The ideal marginals obey `sum_j v_jz A_j = sum_k v_kz B_k = 0`. Consequently,

```text
sum_j v_jz A^epsilon_j = (4 epsilon alpha q1/3) K
sum_k v_kz B^epsilon_k = (4 epsilon alpha q2/3) K.
```

Both perturbed operator systems contain `K`, so their intersection is no longer only scalars. This is a general archive-POVM perturbation, not a claim that this perturbation follows from the fixed D.1 Hamiltonian or from an allowed microscopic error model. It does not show that every perturbation destroys the result, that the two systems become equal, or that operational non-simulability cannot be robust. It shows why an unspecified approximation is insufficient to inherit the exact intersection conclusion.

**Action:** specify the allowed approximation/error class and the operational property to be retained. For example, require a quantitative separation from classical postprocessing on stated finite-energy preparations, with a positive statistical margin and an error budget for the labelled instrument. Exact scalar intersection may instead be retained under separately justified structural restrictions. Add this requirement to the next-gate sentence; a new robustness theorem is not necessary merely to publish the current conditional result.

### 2. [P3] Replace "passive apparatus class" with the actual symmetry assumptions

**Status: Resolved on 19 October 2026.** Verified in D.5, current PDF page 41, with the same current hash. "Symmetry-restricted apparatus class" replaces the disputed phrase while retaining the stated assumptions. Original finding follows.

**Location:** D.5, page 41, immediately after the standalone-detector example.

Stationarity is not thermodynamic passivity. For example, an apparatus with `H_A = diag(0,1)` and state `xi = diag(0,1)` is stationary, but a level swap lowers its energy from one to zero. It has extractable work despite having no energy coherence. The stated assumptions include such nonpassive states.

The obstruction is a time-translation-symmetry obstruction: additive energy conservation, a stationary apparatus preparation, and an energy-commuting labelled pointer force invariant induced effects. Its proof does not need thermodynamic passivity. Calling this a passive class risks identifying the missing resource with energy supply rather than the relevant asymmetry/reference resource.

**Action:** replace the phrase with "this stationary, additive-energy-conserving apparatus class with an energy-commuting pointer," or an equally explicit symmetry-based description. The invariance argument itself need not change.

## Physics Audit

### Effective Writer Versus Microscopic Instrument

**Pages 37-38 and 40-41:** the PDF correctly stipulates the eight labelled jumps, rather than deriving them from `H_FD`. Their boundedness establishes a well-defined effective dissipative perturbation; it does not establish a finite-resource reservoir realization. A time-independent generator establishes time homogeneity of this effective writer, not thermodynamic closure or a derivation of the measurement hardware.

The distinction between effects and instruments is handled correctly. `M_j^* M_j = F_j` fixes the probabilities but not the state update. The reset instrument on page 40 has the same effects and a different nonselective channel. Its scalar second marginal is a valid counterexample to an instrument-independent claim, not evidence that the stipulated Luders instrument is impossible. Matching an unlabelled reduced master equation, a decay rate, or the first POVM would not establish the required outcome-labelled instrument.

The following physical inputs remain supplied rather than derived:

- **Initial state and phase:** the detector starts in `|+x>`, factorized from the input field as encoded by `iota`, with blank registers and the clock at level 2. Its coherence relative to the transverse readout axes is a resource. As an immediate control, replacing this preparation by `I/2` while keeping the stipulated Luders instrument and pure-dephasing evolution makes both marginal field POVMs scalar: no detector off-diagonal blocks are present or generated by the nonselective channel. This observation concerns relative preparation/readout phase, not a claim that a common change of phase convention destroys physics.
- **Energy:** the jump changes detector and interaction energy in general. For a finite-energy joint state, the page-38 nonselective channel gives the expectation-value identity `Delta <H_FD> = -(2/3) <I tensor H_D + g Phi(h) tensor Z>`. The field's free-energy term is unchanged at the instantaneous detector operation. Clock/register energies and reservoir exchange must complete this balance. Its sign is state-dependent; this is not a universal positive work-cost bound.
- **Reservoir and frequency structure:** a derivation must specify spectral densities, initial bath/reference states, correlation times, the approximation regime, and reverse processes. The fixed transverse matrices cannot simply be called spontaneous decay operators without showing the required labelled dynamics. Energy resolution or a change of approximation can change the instrument even if some rates agree.
- **Memory:** orthogonal blank/outcome states, diagonal register Hamiltonians, no overwrite, and one-way progression to a terminal counter state are assumptions. Two retained records do not supply a reusable memory or an indefinitely operating clock. Reinitialization, preparation, stability and resource replenishment remain unmodelled. Page 41's state-independent absorbing erasure is not a general material-memory noise model.
- **Spatial realization:** a continuum Klein-Gordon field and smooth spatial smearing are not a derivation of a localized relativistic apparatus. The fixed profile and Hamiltonian select an apparatus frame and spatial placement. The internal two-level scalar avoids identifying Bloch axes with physical spatial spin, but does not establish a covariant material detector, gravitational observables, or an AQFT local-algebra selection rule.

These are physical-realization gates. The present PDF generally identifies them as assumptions, so their absence should not be relabelled as a discovered contradiction in the conditional calculation.

### Conservation Obstruction

**Pages 40-41:** the stated group-symmetry argument is sound under its assumptions. Commuting the joint time translation through the energy-conserving unitary and invariant pointer, then using apparatus stationarity, makes `G_j` invariant under system time translations. The tetrahedral effects are not invariant for the standalone `H_D = Omega Z/2` with `Omega > 0`.

Crucial qualifications are already present and should remain: this is not a no-go for all apparatuses; a time-independent interacting Hamiltonian does not imply conservation of the chosen additive bare energy; and the standalone detector Hamiltonian must not silently replace the full interacting system energy. A reference carrying asymmetry, a different pointer model, or different conservation assumptions changes the premises. The restricted result cannot certify impossibility of all microscopic implementations.

### Ordinary Time, Retained Archives and Algebras

**Pages 38-40:** `t` and the common observation horizon `T` are ordinary apparatus-time parameters. The counter's state-independent waiting-time process does not derive Page-Wootters conditioning, a stationary constrained universe, or a thermodynamic arrow. The preparation and terminal readout protocol must not disappear from an eventual autonomous-realization claim.

The different weights `gamma exp(-gamma t)` and `gamma^2 t exp(-gamma t)` compare first and second retained records. The separate `1/3` coefficient is detector backaction, not the source of their nonproportional temporal weighting. The same horizon and missing-event effects prevent an illicit comparison based on postselecting completed runs.

Both marginals are classical coarse-grainings of the same 21-outcome final archive. Neither being a classical postprocessing of the other does not make them incompatible quantum measurements. There is no loss of the first record, no demonstrated shrinking of total accessible information, and no replacement of one operational description by another. "Complete archive" here means the specified terminal count-and-label register readout, not all possible environmental or time-resolved records.

The scalar intersection concerns finite-dimensional linear operator systems. It neither identifies nor separates their generated von Neumann algebras. It also does not characterize the full set of measurements attainable with arbitrary controls on this hardware. The finite-energy witnesses prove existence for selected input states; they do not give a uniform preparation-energy budget, experimental sample complexity, or a cosmological observable.

### No Entropy or ICC Conclusion

**Pages 4, 6-8, 12-13, 24-25 and 37-41:** Appendix D has not instantiated the older hypothesis's stationary pure global state, intrinsic selector, selected clock/rest decomposition, entropy functional or aeonic boundary relation. Its prepared open apparatus and arbitrary input-field states are a separate task. An effective nonunitary subsystem law is not a contradiction of global unitarity, but neither is a dilation of this specific apparatus into the proposed stationary cosmology supplied.

No entropy ordering follows from different operator systems or their scalar intersection. In particular, no restricted von Neumann entropy of a selected local factor, observational entropy contrast with specified volume factors, gravitational entropy, low-entropy boundary, or repeatable cycle is established. Keeping the two records actually rules out interpreting this example as the disappearance of the earlier record. Even a successful laboratory implementation would demonstrate an ordinary sequential measurement effect unless an additional, independently justified cosmological connection were supplied.

## Primary-Citation Check

Only the three requested primary papers were used for the new literature check. These pinpointed claims support the limited uses in D.5; none supplies ICC evidence.

1. **Woods, arXiv:2005.04628v3:** Section 5, Proposition 2, equations (21a)-(21c), gives the registered semigroup representation. 2. **Trushechkin, arXiv:2103.12042v3:** Section II assumes a purely discrete system spectrum and a product initial state with stationary bath; equation (4) resolves coupling operators by Bohr frequency. Appendix A additionally specifies discrete spectra, bounded interaction operators and an integrability condition on bath correlations before its convergence theorem. This is not a direct theorem for the complete continuum `H_FD` construction. [Primary text, Sections II-III and Appendix A](https://arxiv.org/html/2103.12042v3).
3. **Mohammady, Miyadera and Loveridge, arXiv:2110.11705v3:** Proposition 3.1, equation (14), bounds the effect commutator using error and apparatus quantum Fisher information under full conservation. Equation (13) and its following discussion identify zero apparatus asymmetry with commutation of its state and conserved quantity. Exact measurement with an invariant pointer and zero apparatus asymmetry then requires invariant system effects. This supports the restricted reasoning, not a general prohibition of decay-based detectors. The paper's bounded conserved-quantity setting is not itself a theorem for an unbounded continuum Hamiltonian. [Primary text, Section 3.2](https://arxiv.org/html/2110.11705v3#S3.SS2).
