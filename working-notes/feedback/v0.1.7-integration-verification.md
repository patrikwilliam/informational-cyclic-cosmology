## Scope of Assurance

The matrix runs are diagnostics, not proofs of infinite-dimensional claims.
Scoped Lean components are distinguished from the analytic bridges in the
manuscript and the proof-project README. The finite-access comparison assumes
external preparation/calibration while restricting the candidate effect sets.
The separate mathematical review checks the explicit analytic arguments;
the physics review checks their interpretation and stated limits. Neither
establishes a cosmological mechanism, supplies the missing physical input,
proves novelty, or replaces independent expert review. Historical calculations
were preserved and selected runners were rerun, not all older theorems and
large lattice searches re-audited.
