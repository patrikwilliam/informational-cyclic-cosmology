## Mathematical Corrections

- **M1:** the manuscript now distinguishes one-particle $U(8)$, its
  number-preserving lift $\Gamma(V)$, and unrestricted $U(256)$ on Fock space.
  A generic eight-mode lift built with the existing executable implementation
  commuted with total particle number exactly in the numerical check; its
  maximum unitarity error was $2.00\times10^{-15}$. The subgroup distinction
  follows from number preservation, not from numerical sampling.
- **M2:** the supporting analysis now requires separation under the full
  quotient, not merely a state-changing energy-degeneracy rotation. The
  explicit controlled rotation $R_\theta=W_\theta V_\theta$ was checked at
  three angles: it changes the state but is quotient-equivalent to the
  reference, with zero cost and unit reduced purity. Maximum numerical error
  was $1.11\times10^{-16}$. The added analytic zero-cost classification for
  $H=Z\otimes I_2$, $\rho=|00\rangle\langle00|$ supplies a counterexample to
  the former universal degeneracy inference. It does not refute the
  nondegenerate-energy proposition, which is unchanged.
- **M3:** the two-cell simultaneous strictness window is explicit. For
  $D=1+16r^2$, the tangent Hessian eigenvalues are
  $(24r^2,24r^2-2)/D$ at the site endpoint and
  $(2,2-12r^2)/D$ at the momentum endpoint. They are both positive exactly for
  $1/\sqrt{12}<|r|<1/\sqrt6$. Finite differences at six couplings, including
  both bounds, zero, and $1/3$, agreed to within $10^{-6}$. At $r=1/3$ the
  endpoint costs coincide and both minimum Hessian eigenvalues are $0.24$.
- **M4:** the label for $A$ is now a squared Hilbert-Schmidt norm, matching the
  existing closure definition and coefficient $A=109824$. No coefficient was
  recalculated or changed for this wording correction.

The Gaussian-rate identity was additionally checked in 32 seeded random
Hamiltonian/factorization examples in dimensions $2\times2$, $2\times3$,
$3\times2$, and $3\times3$ (NumPy generator seed 20260914). An independent
orthonormal span projector onto the transported local operator subspaces was
compared with the partial-trace projection formula. The maximum discrepancy
in $C_H=d\tau_s^{-2}/\|H-(\operatorname{Tr}H/d)I\|_2^2$ was
$5.56\times10^{-16}$. These are normalization checks of an established
identity, not new scrambling dynamics or an optimization campaign.

## Physical Interpretation

- **F1:** the plus-sign functional is explicitly a positive trajectory
  penalty of Euclidean form, not a stable real-time action. Its real-time
  Euler-Lagrange equation would have the unstable sign near a cost minimum.
  The downhill law requires a separate dissipative assumption and positive
  coefficients; the clock, parameter, and branch-memory interpretation remain
  unproved.
- **F2:** the density inference now requires a physical cell size and fixed
  nonzero mean occupation. The relation
  $r\sim\kappa(n_{\mathrm{phys}}/\bar N)^{2/3}$ follows conditionally from
  $n_{\mathrm{phys}}=\bar N/\ell^3$, not from regulator refinement alone.
  The Planckian-cutoff warning and absence of a physical reset-density
  prediction remain explicit.
- **F3:** [Zanardi et al. (2024)](https://arxiv.org/html/2212.14340), equations
  (14) and (16), were checked against the manuscript normalization. For fixed
  non-scalar $H$, the squared Gaussian rate and interaction cost differ only
  by a positive candidate-independent multiplier on the finite bipartite
  factor-algebra domain. The paper and linked problem statements now treat
  this as direct prior art, not an independent escape from the same
  obstruction. Neither an extension to charge-sector or continuum algebras
  nor a result for finite-time scrambling is asserted.
- **F4:** the active open questions acknowledge the completed finite branch.
  The three-cell note retains its historical date while linking the later
  four-cell gates and closure. Its calculations are not presented as a
  boundary-independent, large-size, or cosmological result.

A separate bounded physics wording check found no substantiated residual issue
within these corrected F1-F4 passages. Summary cross-checks also removed stale
minimal-scrambling escape language from the linked two-qubit and general
no-go notes. No claim of full physical viability follows from these checks.
