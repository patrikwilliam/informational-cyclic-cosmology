# Mathematical Audit of ICC v0.1.3

Date: 18 July 2026

Status: internal pre-publication audit, not independent peer review

Scope update, 10 August 2026: this audit covers the original two-cell v0.1.3
increment. The later three- and four-cell additions have their own separate
reviews. The final four-cell onsite classification is audited in
[`four-cell-onsite-closure-mathematical-review.md`](./four-cell-onsite-closure-mathematical-review.md).

## Verdict

No release-blocking mathematical error was found in the v0.1.3 finite
increment after the centered-normalization correction recorded in the
adversarial review.

The following statements survive the audit under their stated assumptions:

1. the Hilbert-Schmidt map used in the selector is the orthogonal projection
   onto Kronecker-sum Hamiltonians;
2. the unrestricted strict-minimum argument excludes an entangled isolated
   eigenstate at a strict local minimum of that selector;
3. the normalizer of $I_2\otimes M_4(\mathbb C)$ has the stated product form;
4. the restricted candidate space is the projective plane
   $\mathbb{RP}^2\simeq S^2/\{\pm1\}$;
5. the full-Fock and fixed-$N=4$ branch diagrams, transition couplings,
   spinodals, and barrier values follow from the displayed costs;
6. the exact certificate proves simplicity of the positive-coupling
   full-Fock ground state and the stated lower bound on its spectral gap; and
7. the sector-resolved entropy calculation is internally consistent for the
   fixed-total-number pure states used in the model.

The audit required wording corrections about full-Fock sector weighting, the
sign of the coupling at which stationary states were evaluated, the exact
candidate-space quotient, and the scope of the superselection diagnostic.
These corrections do not alter any calculated minimum, coupling, spectrum, or
entropy value.

## 1. Hilbert-Schmidt projection and normalization

For $K\in\mathcal B(\mathcal H_A\otimes\mathcal H_B)$, let

$$
\mathfrak L=\{A\otimes I_B+I_A\otimes B\}.
$$

The manuscript uses

$$
P_{\mathrm{loc}}(K)=
\frac{\operatorname{Tr}_B K}{d_B}\otimes I_B
+I_A\otimes\frac{\operatorname{Tr}_A K}{d_A}
-\frac{\operatorname{Tr}K}{d_Ad_B}I.
$$

The residual $R=K-P_{\mathrm{loc}}(K)$ has
$\operatorname{Tr}_A R=\operatorname{Tr}_B R=0$. It is therefore
Hilbert-Schmidt orthogonal to every element of $\mathfrak L$. The map is
idempotent and is the claimed orthogonal projection. The scalar subtraction in
the denominator is necessary because the identity lies in $\mathfrak L$.

The corrected full-Fock denominator is

$$
512+8192r^2,
$$

and the corrected fixed-sector denominator is

$$
160+\frac{15744}{7}r^2.
$$

Both agree with the centered selector definition. Since either denominator is
independent of the candidate orientation, centering changes normalized cost
values but not the optimizer geometry.

## 2. Unrestricted quotient argument

Let $K=U^\dagger HU$, $L=P_{\mathrm{loc}}K$, and
$J=(I-P_{\mathrm{loc}})K$. A variation $Ue^{tX}$ with $X^\dagger=-X$ gives

$$
\dot K=[K,X].
$$

Stationarity of $\|J\|_2^2$ for every $X$ implies $[K,L]=0$. A simple
eigenvector of $K$ is consequently an eigenvector of $L$. If the corresponding
$L$-eigenvalue is nondegenerate, the eigenvector is one product eigenvector of
the Kronecker sum $A\otimes I+I\otimes B$.

If the state is entangled, its $L$-eigenspace must instead be degenerate. For
any unitary $R$ supported in that eigenspace,

$$
\|(I-P_{\mathrm{loc}})(R^\dagger K R)\|_2^2
=\|J\|_2^2-\|P_{\mathrm{loc}}(R^\dagger J R)\|_2^2
\leq\|J\|_2^2.
$$

The reduced-state purity is invariant under the stated quotient equivalences.
An analytic unitary path inside the degenerate eigenspace can change that
purity from its entangled value to one while never increasing the cost.
Real-analyticity supplies quotient-inequivalent points arbitrarily close to the
starting point. This correctly excludes a strict local minimum.

The argument is valid in arbitrary finite bipartite dimension under the stated
simple-eigenvalue and unrestricted-unitary assumptions. It says nothing by
itself about mixed states, degenerate selected energy levels, restricted CAR
families, or non-entanglement definitions of effective entropy.

## 3. Normalizer and candidate quotient

Suppose

$$
V(I_2\otimes M_4)V^\dagger=I_2\otimes M_4.
$$

Conjugation by $V$ induces an automorphism of $M_4(\mathbb C)$. After removing
its inner implementation $S\in U(4)$, the remaining unitary commutes with
$I_2\otimes M_4$ and hence lies in its commutant $M_2\otimes I_4$. Thus

$$
V=W\otimes S.
$$

The unordered two-cell decompositions induced by $W$ form

$$
U(2)/\left((U(1)\times U(1))\rtimes S_2\right)
\simeq\mathbb{RP}^2
\simeq S^2/\{\pm1\}.
$$

This establishes the mathematical parameterization. It does not derive the
normalizer restriction from Einstein-Cartan dynamics. That remains a defining
assumption of the finite model.

## 4. Full-Fock branch calculation

After minimizing over $n_x$ and setting $u=n_z^2$, the unnormalized numerator
is

$$
N(u;r)=u+3r^2(3-2u-u^2),\qquad 0\leq u\leq1.
$$

For $r\ne0$, $N''=-6r^2<0$, so a global minimum lies at an endpoint. The
endpoint exchange is $9r^2=1$, hence $|r_c|=1/3$.

The tangent quadratic coefficients are

$$
N_{\mathrm{mom}}=9r^2+x^2+(1-6r^2)z^2+O(4),
$$

$$
N_{\mathrm{site}}=1+12r^2x^2+(12r^2-1)y^2+O(4).
$$

They give the stated coexistence interval
$1/\sqrt{12}<|r|<1/\sqrt6$. At $r^2=1/9$, the minimax path must cross
$u=1/2$, where the corrected normalized values are

$$
C_{\min}=\frac9{25},\qquad
C_{\mathrm{barrier}}=\frac{39}{100},\qquad
\Delta C=\frac3{100}.
$$

The selector geometry depends on $r^2$. The exact spectral and numerical
entropy calculations supplied in v0.1.3 use the positive branch $r=1/3$ only.

## 5. Fixed-$N=4$ gate

The charge-block dimensions are $1,16,36,16,1$. In block $k$, projection onto
$A_k\otimes I+I\otimes B_{4-k}$ has dimension
$d_k^2+d_{4-k}^2-1$. Summing gives

$$
1+31+71+31+1=135,
$$

as stated. The blockwise partial-trace formula is the same orthogonal
projection proved in Section 1, and off-diagonal local-charge blocks are
orthogonal to the chosen number-preserving local subspace.

With $u=n_z^2$, the numerator is

$$
N_4(u;r)=160u+24r^2(1-u)(71+25u).
$$

It is concave for $r\ne0$. Endpoint equality gives
$r_c^2=20/213$. The tangent coefficients give

$$
\sqrt{\frac5{72}}<|r|<\sqrt{\frac{10}{69}},
$$

and direct substitution at $u=1/2$ gives the displayed minimum and barrier
fractions. The stationary-state calculation is numerical and uses the positive
branch $r=+\sqrt{20/213}$.

The full-Fock trace does not weight particle-number sectors equally. It is the
sum of sector traces; expressed using normalized sector traces, sector $N$ has
weight $\binom8N/256$. The fixed-$N=4$ calculation is therefore a change of
weighting, not the removal of an equal average over sectors.

## 6. Exact spectral certificate

The certificate constructs the real integer matrix for $3H_c$ from exact CAR
data. Exact block diagonalization and rational $LDL^\mathsf T$ inertia show
that precisely one eigenvalue lies below $-21/2$. The characteristic
polynomial of that block contains the stated quartic, whose low root is
bracketed by exact rational sign evaluations. Since only one eigenvalue,
counting multiplicity, lies below the threshold, that root is simple and is the
global ground eigenvalue.

The next eigenvalue is at least $-21/2$ in the spectrum of $3H_c$. Combining
this with the upper endpoint of the ground-root bracket gives

$$
E_1-E_0>\frac{923}{1500}.
$$

The certificate therefore earns the words "exactly certified" for the
positive-branch ground-state simplicity and gap bound. It does not certify the
reported entropy values.

## 7. Entropy and superselection

The raw values are fermionic mode-entanglement entropies for a fixed
Jordan-Wigner ordering and specified mode bipartition. Both stationary ground
states used in the controls have fixed total particle number. Their local
reduced states are consequently block diagonal in local particle number.

For local sector projectors $P_s$, the calculated quantity is

$$
E_{\mathrm{SSR}}=\sum_s p_s
S(P_s\rho_A P_s/p_s),\qquad
p_s=\operatorname{Tr}(P_s\rho_A).
$$

For these block-diagonal states,

$$
S(\rho_A)=H(\{p_s\})+E_{\mathrm{SSR}}.
$$

The implementation now asserts this identity for both the local-number and
local-parity decompositions. The quantity is appropriately described as a
sector-resolved pure-state diagnostic. It should not be advertised as a
general mixed-state fermionic entanglement measure or as thermodynamic entropy.

## 8. Reproduction

The audit reran the following with Python 3 and NumPy:

- `research/ec_axial_selector_analysis.py`, including 12 random orientations
  and 121 bistability samples;
- `research/ec_fixed_sector_selector.py`, including 12 random orientations and
  241 bistability samples;
- `research/ec_ground_state_certificate.py`;
- `research/ec_selector_adversarial_controls.py`;
- `research/ec_superselection_controls.py`;
- `research/two_qubit_selector_analysis.py` with 64 starts and all product
  permutations; and
- `research/two_by_three_selector_analysis.py`.

All completed successfully. The largest reported closed-form residual errors
were approximately $1.3\times10^{-11}$ in full Fock space and
$2.1\times10^{-12}$ in the fixed four-particle sector. These floating-point
checks support the closed forms but are not substitutes for the exact spectral
certificate.

## Release recommendation

The finite mathematics is sufficiently coherent for release as a versioned
conceptual preprint, provided the manuscript keeps its present qualifications:

- the unrestricted proposition is not externally peer reviewed;
- the admissible CAR manifold is assumed;
- only the positive-coupling stationary states were tested;
- the entropy contrasts outside the exact spectral statement are numerical;
- the two-cell geometry is not Einstein-Cartan specific; and
- no continuum selector or cosmological result follows from the finite model.
