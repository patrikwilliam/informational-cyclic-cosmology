# Physics Review: Capability-Set Comparison

15 October 2026. Separate internal AI-assisted interpretation review.

## Verdict

The finite operational claim is supported under explicit conditions: after
unread priming, loss of access to the old input leaves a reference resource
whose attainable phase-sensitive measurements are strictly weaker within the
same symmetric processing class. Improving the particular readout recovers
some, but not all, of its former performance. This is not just a relabeling or
a reference-phase rotation, and no entropy objective is used.

## What the Result Adds

The earlier calculation only compared two effects from one protocol. The new
bound applies to every invariant joint measurement on the retained DM apparatus
and a fresh input. It includes more control than the actual setup has. Since
even this broad after ceiling falls below an implemented before score, missing
hardware controls cannot invalidate the narrow separation claim. An exact
optimization over exposure times for the original H and n_D gives the same
qualitative conclusion without adding a pointer axis or a new interaction.

## Assumptions That Must Stay Visible

1. **Access is specified, not derived.** The old input and any preparation
   label or purification of its I/2 state are unavailable. Its disappearance
   from the accessible resource is not established by an autonomous spacetime
   transport model. The result does not justify this boundary for ICC.
2. **The symmetry restriction is operational.** No additional phase reference,
   asymmetric control drive, or trained coherent probe is available. Total
   charge conservation by a Hamiltonian alone would not forbid an experimenter
   from bringing such resources. Those resources are explicitly excluded here.
3. **All invariant measurements are a benchmark, not derived controls.** The
   optimum is exact in that broad class. It is not a claim that the fixed
   Hamiltonian implements the optimal joint measurement. Its role as an upper
   bound remains valid for narrower symmetric hardware access.
4. **Compare equal resources and unconditional statistics.** One retained
   apparatus and one unknown input per trial. No fresh reference, many-copy
   processing, conditional-only success score, or reused known coherent input
   is hidden in the optimization. The label preparation apparatus is external
   to the detector's resource budget.
5. **Full access changes the conclusion.** With the old input retained and
   arbitrary invariant joint measurements, the before/after capability sets
   are equal by unitary conjugation. The local loss does not destroy global
   asymmetry or information. With unrestricted direct Y measurements the
   reference is unnecessary and both task scores are 1.
6. **Record formation is still not the explanation.** The mixed-memory control
   has no local input-label record but the same optimum for this phase task.
   Correlations and accessible charge coherence matter; this does not select
   an observer algebra or make records universally irrelevant.
7. **Precision is not a new algebra.** Some phase access survives after priming.
   Both full symmetric effect sets span M_2, despite their strict set inclusion.
   The result is about attainable measurement strengths and statistics, not a
   new subsystem structure or the disappearance of a whole observable sector.

## Implication for ICC

This establishes a limited distinction between changing protocol performance
and genuinely changing accessible measurement capability. The former alone
was insufficient evidence; the latter now has a finite conditional witness.
The supplied tensor factors, Q symmetry, apparatus interaction, clock, fresh
input supply and accessibility boundary remain essential. The result does
not provide a physically selected factorization, an aeonic boundary, a cyclic
history, or an entropy reset. No reinterpretation of the manuscript follows.

The relevant principle is established finite-reference/resource physics, not
an asserted new axiom. The work is suitable as a documented conditional example
with its limitation, not as new observational evidence for ICC. No fourth-cell
or larger dynamical calculation, release change, or further interpretation is
part of this check.
