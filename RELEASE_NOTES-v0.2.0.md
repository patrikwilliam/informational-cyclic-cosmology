# Version 0.2.0

Prepared 19 October 2026. No publication or new version-specific DOI is
asserted. The author's ORCID and all-versions DOI are unchanged.

## Main Addition

Appendix D introduces a conditional continuum field-detector model with
outcome-bearing ticks and a complete 21-outcome retained archive. Under the
specified tetrahedral Luders instrument its first and second marginal
operator systems intersect only in scalars. This goes beyond changing
visibility within one operator system, but does not establish different
generated von Neumann algebras. Both marginals are compatible readouts of
one archive, not successive cosmological descriptions.

## Counterexample and Verification

- A fixed alternative instrument has the same first-outcome POVM but makes
  the second field marginal scalar. Agreement of effects alone is insufficient.
- Two small finite matrix methods cross-check the record and marginal formulas.
  Their agreement is numerical, not a certified continuum limit.
- A pinned Lean package verifies concrete detector identities, the same-POVM
  counterexample and a conditional span-intersection implication. Its 32
  declarations include helper lemmas, not 32 new scientific results.
- Continuum independence, tensor extension, clock-field dynamics and physical
  resource accounting are not end-to-end Lean formalized.
- Woods (2021), Trushechkin (2021), and Mohammady, Miyadera and Loveridge (2023)
  are cited for relevant frameworks and limitations, not ICC endorsement or
  a supplied microscopic realization.

## What Has Not Changed

Core postulates, previous results and CC BY 4.0 licensing remain unchanged.

are unresolved. The version bump identifies this new branch of the research
