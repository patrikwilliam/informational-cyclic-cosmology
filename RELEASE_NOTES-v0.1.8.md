## Main Addition

With the old input inaccessible and the same charge-symmetric processing
class on both sides, unread priming produces a proper inclusion of attainable
measurement-effect sets. The original before effect is outside the entire
after benchmark: its success is 0.7197744539 versus the after optimum
0.6813596618. Analytic formulas, duration optimization and limiting controls
are in `research/v018/`.

## What Has Not Changed

Both capability sets span the same `M_2` algebra. Keeping the old input and
granting full symmetric joint control restores equality. A mixed-memory
control prevents interpreting the effect as evidence that record formation
selects an algebra. This is no cosmological transition, entropy reset, new
spatial dimension, new interpretation of aeons or physical selector.

Core postulates, earlier negative results, the closed reference-phase-only
route and the broader open selector problem remain unchanged. The methods
have established prior art; no unverified AI-generated theorem is a premise.
The manuscript, README, citation metadata, open questions and formatted PDF
are synchronized to v0.1.8. Older release PDFs and archives are preserved.
