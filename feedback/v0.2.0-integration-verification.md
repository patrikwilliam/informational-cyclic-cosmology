## Separate PDF Reviews and Corrections

The [mathematical review](v0.2.0-pdf-mathematical-review.md) and
[physics review](v0.2.0-pdf-physics-review.md) were assigned to separate AI
reviewers with fresh task context, starting from the generated PDF rather than
the draft source. They are not independent external expert peer review.
Their scope and final-file identification are recorded in each report.
Both reviewers re-extracted the corrected PDF and verified the final hash
above. The mathematics addendum found the changes accurate; the physics
follow-up marked both findings resolved. Neither identified a remaining
publication-blocking issue within its bounded scope.

The mathematical pass found no substantive error in the bounded increment.
The physics pass requested two clarifications, applied in D.5 before the
final recheck:

1. The next implementation task now requires a declared error class and an
   operational success criterion. Small effect errors alone do not preserve
   exact scalar-only operator-system intersection. No new robustness theorem
   or measured separation margin is claimed.
2. "Passive apparatus" was replaced with "symmetry-restricted apparatus."
   Stationarity is not thermodynamic passivity; the stated conservation and
   pointer assumptions, rather than a passivity assumption, do the work.

The instrument, all equations and the formal proof sources are unchanged by
these corrections. Microscopic realization, generated-algebra selection,
relational dynamics, cosmological entropy and repeatability remain open.
