## Findings and Corrections

1. **Embedded algebra versus isomorphism.** Prime occupation coordinates alone
   identify the untwisted factor. The reference-dependent factor additionally
   requires the diagonal phase conjugation. The supplement and appendix now
   state this explicitly instead of allowing an identification that omits D.
2. **Stationarity with an unbounded Hamiltonian.** The reviewed statement uses
   invariance under exp(-itH), rather than an unqualified operator commutator
   with H. The simple, multiplicity-one logarithmic spectrum is explicit.
3. **Distance convention.** The supplement now defines the full closed-unit-ball
   Hausdorff distance in operator norm. Its maximum is 1 in this convention;
   the witness is not merely a distance between two chosen generators.
4. **Finite boundaries and absent transitions.** Compressed shifts are partial
   isometries. Additional checks verify their initial projections, zero-weight
   transitions, and the energy-exchange formula only on the allowed domain.

These are precision and coverage corrections. No error was found in the central
conditional construction or the scoped obstructions after these corrections.

## Analytic Checks

- Covariance maps the one-dimensional energy space at log(n) into that at
  log(mn). Isometry fixes the coefficient modulus. Applying the semigroup
  identity to |1> gives alpha_m(n) = eta_mn / eta_n, so all compatible full
  families are related by a diagonal unitary. This uses every stated premise.
- The frequency component of a bounded probe is a bounded orthogonal weighted
  shift, with a strongly convergent sum. Nonzero weights give an isometric
  polar factor even without a lower singular-value bound. Missing weights do
  not define a full isometry.
- A rank-one full-support reference gives a telescoping phase ratio and hence
  exact multiplication. A general probe need not satisfy the square relations;
  separately extracted composites need their own consistency check.
- The unilateral shift generates the full type-I factor via its vacuum
  projection and matrix units. Twisting by one common diagonal unitary
  preserves commutation of different prime factors.
- A normal stationary density operator is diagonal. It is invariant under
  the comparison unitary, so the restricted-state spectra and entropy agree.
  Equality at infinite entropy is not a subtraction of infinities. Neither
  pure states nor Gibbs states are needed for the general equality.
- Free reference evolution multiplies each shift by one scalar; its generated
  algebra is unchanged.
- The reference with coefficients sqrt(6)/(pi n) is normalized and has finite
  mean logarithmic energy. A far-out odd-index phase flip has reference trace
  distance tending to zero. Comparing two occupation fibers bounds distance
  of the conjugated shift from the entire original factor below by 1; zero
  gives the matching upper bound. This establishes the full KK claim.

## Pre-Integration Fixed-Access Review

A fresh bounded mathematical pass, before integration, found no substantive
blocker to Appendix B.4 and the [operational supplement](../research/v017/operational-stability.md).
The following were checked separately from the physical interpretation:

- The common midpoint gives effect-set Hausdorff distance at most 1/2. The
  fixed two-probe witness gives the matching lower bound for every N >= 6.
- The exact geometric sums give normalized full-support references with a
  shared mean-energy ceiling below 2 and trace distance tending to zero. Their
  mean energies need not be constant across epsilon.
- Their limit is outside the full-support domain. This disproves uniform
  continuity without disproving pointwise continuity in the finite-window metric.
- Global phase alignment and centering effects at half-identity give
  min(1/2, 2 delta/mu_N(c)) in both Hausdorff directions. No optimality of the
  coefficient is claimed.
- The original fixed-reference full-norm discontinuity is unchanged: its
  high-index flips eventually leave each fixed window and become invisible.

The [Lean project](../research/v017/lean/README.md) audits 16 declarations across
the B.3 and B.4 components. It does not formalize polar reconstruction,
reference energy/normalization sums, trace distance, the exact compressed-set
Hausdorff statement or the assembled local-continuity theorem. The numerical
checks are diagnostics, not replacements for those analytic arguments.

**Disposition:** suitable for inclusion as a bounded mathematical limitation
and conditioning result, not an ICC no-go or successful selector. Internal
AI-assisted review is not independent human peer review.
