## Mathematical Checks

### 1. Hamiltonian and Exact Propagator: No Issue Found

**PDF pp. 37 and 39, D.1-D.2.** Positive mass and smooth compact spatial smearing supply the stated one-particle integrability. The field perturbation has the usual infinitesimal free-Hamiltonian bound, and the energy-form bound supplies a lower bound. No massless, pointlike, or infinite-time limit is needed for the stated result.

I checked the signs and normalization using the PDF's antilinear-first inner product and Weyl convention. In the interaction picture, set

```text
f'_t = g exp(i omega t) u,
V_s(t) = exp(i theta(t)) W(-s f_t).
```

Differentiating the Weyl product gives

```text
V'_s V_s* = -i s phi(f'_t)
             + i [theta'(t) - Im<f_t,f'_t>/2] I.

Im<f_t,f'_t>/2
  = (g^2/2) integral |u(k)|^2 [1-cos(omega_k t)]/omega_k d^3k.
```

This is precisely the derivative of the printed `theta(t)`. Thus the positive scalar phase in the propagator is required, and it is common to both detector sectors. It cancels in `U_+(t)* U_-(t)`, giving the printed `exp(i Omega t) W(2 f_t)`. No missing phase or factor of two was found. The use of strong-vector/ultraweak integrals, rather than an assumption of operator-norm continuity of Weyl operators, is appropriate.

### 2. Complete Archive and Sequential Marginals: No Issue Found

**PDF pp. 37-39, D.1-D.2.** The active-sector sum of jump squares is `gamma I`, including after a first labelled jump. Consequently the exactly-one-tick weight is `gamma exp(-gamma T)`, while the two-tick density is `gamma^2 exp(-gamma t2)`. There is no additional survival factor after the second tick because the clock is then inactive.

Summing the displayed positive effects gives

```text
sum_j E_1j = gamma T exp(-gamma T) I,
sum_jk E_2jk = [1-exp(-gamma T)(1+gamma T)] I.
```

Together with `E_0`, these normalize the 21-outcome archive. Integrating over the second event recovers the first-event weight `gamma exp(-gamma t1)`. The missing-event outcomes have the stated scalar effects; neither marginal silently postselects successful runs.

The tetrahedral channel is `(X + tr(X) I)/3`. Extending this finite detector identity to field-valued blocks multiplies the off-diagonal blocks by `1/3`. The two diagonal-block traces remain equal for the specified initial detector state. After propagation from `t1` to `t2`, the off-diagonal block is exactly one third of the uninterrupted block at `t2`, independently of `t1`. This justifies both the `gamma^2 t exp(-gamma t)` time weight and the separate denominator `12` in `B_k`. Omitting either distinction would be wrong; the PDF retains both.

### 3. Continuum Independence: No Issue Found

**PDF pp. 39-40, D.3.** I checked the potentially delicate steps rather than treating the finite diagnostics as evidence for this theorem:

- Nonzero radial smooth smearing yields an interval of frequencies with nonzero profile. If `f_s = c f_t`, division by that profile yields an equality of the entire functions `(exp(i z s)-1)/(i z)` and `c(exp(i z t)-1)/(i z)` on an interval. Their removable values at zero and first derivatives give `s=c t` and `s^2=c t^2`; positive times force `s=t` and `c=1`. Thus both arcs are injective, and their positive-time portions cannot meet each other or zero.
- Their union is compact in the one-particle Hilbert-space norm. The coherent-state expectation has the printed phase sign and Gaussian factor. It defines a finite complex measure on that compact union; no infinite-dimensional Lebesgue measure or unsupported Fourier-inversion formula is being assumed.
- The characters form a unital, conjugation-closed algebra. They separate distinct points: for their nonzero difference `delta`, choosing `q=i lambda delta` and a suitable real `lambda` makes the phases different. Complex Stone-Weierstrass therefore applies on the compact union.
- Vanishing against all characters implies the measure is zero. Although both arcs meet at zero, the time pushforwards have no atom there. The scalar atom is therefore separately zero. The two nonzero arcs are disjoint Borel sets, so their densities vanish separately.
- The Gaussian and phase factors never vanish. The remaining densities force `b+d gamma t=0` and `c+e gamma t=0` almost everywhere on a nontrivial interval, hence all coefficients vanish.

This establishes the stated five-operator independence under D.1's assumptions. The span identification is also justified: explicitly, `C_1=3 sum_j v_jx A_j` and `S_1=3 sum_j v_jy A_j`, while `C_2=9 sum_k v_kx B_k` and `S_2=9 sum_k v_ky B_k`. Identity is included in each completed POVM span.

The scalar intersection excludes classical postprocessing in either direction, but not joint measurability. The common archive supplies that compatibility. It does not establish different generated von Neumann algebras; the PDF correctly stops short of this stronger claim.

### 4. Finite-Energy Witnesses: No Issue Found

**PDF p. 40, final paragraph of D.3.** Matrix-element functionals with both vectors in the dense free-energy form domain separate bounded operators. On the finite-dimensional span in question, finitely many such functionals suffice. Taking the span of their finitely many vectors gives a finite-rank projection whose compression is injective on that operator span. Its range still lies in the form domain.

The compressed marginal span is adjoint-closed and contains `P`. Hilbert-Schmidt projection of a Hermitian effect onto that span is Hermitian; its residual `D` is therefore Hermitian and traceless. The printed epsilon bound keeps both `P/d +/- epsilon D` positive, with trace one. Orthogonality gives equal first-marginal probabilities, and the second-effect difference is exactly `2 epsilon tr(D^2)>0`. The reversed construction works as stated.

Every vector in this finite-dimensional support has finite free-energy form value, so the resulting finite-rank density operators have finite mean free energy. The argument does not supply one uniform energy bound across parameters, an explicit continuum preparation, or a lower bound on the distinguishing signal. Those limitations are acknowledged, not concealed proof premises.
