# v0.1.7 Physics Review

Date: 10 October 2026. Second disciplinary pass, after the mathematical review.

Scope: physical meaning and overclaim risk of the arithmetic increment. Internal
AI-assisted assessment, not independent expert peer review and not a renewed
validation of all earlier ICC physics.

## Findings and Disposition

1. **No independently derived input.** The exact logarithmic spectrum and the
   coherent reference are supplied. This is a research gap, not corrected by
   prime factorization. The appendix and abstract explicitly present conditional
   reconstruction, not a physically derived selector or a new cosmological axiom.
2. **Reference-state data are not automatic operational access.** Knowing a
   projector mathematically does not show that its frequency components or polar
   factors can be implemented. The energy-raising identity requires a specified
   driving or internal energy-exchange mechanism. No free operation is inferred.
3. **Stationary contrast fails in this model.** The compatible embeddings differ
   by state-preserving symmetries, and free reference evolution leaves the
   algebras unchanged. This is a negative result for the declared route, not
   evidence for a cycle. ICC's unspecified constraint need not have this spectrum.
4. **Full-norm instability is not universal physical instability.** The example
   is sensitive to changes in scarcely populated high-energy components. It
   establishes failure in the stated norm, not failure of every finite-access,
   energy-restricted or state-weighted task. Conversely, a weaker metric is
   not automatically justified or shown to meet ICC's needs. The additional
   hard-cutoff analysis below supplies one conditional comparison, not a
   derivation of ICC's operational resources.
5. **Infinite dimension is not a continuum QFT construction.** Prime-occupation
   factors here are type I and have no derived spatial locality, causal net,
   gravitational dressing or cosmological entropy. This limitation is explicit.
6. **Literature supports ingredients, not ICC.** Bost-Connes structure,
   number-field reconstruction and reference-frame resources do not validate
   ICC. The paused OpenAI 290/282 work and Riemann-related manuscripts are not
   ingredients of this release. No external approval or new theorem is implied.

## Strategy

Retain the result as a compact appendix and a reproducible negative/conditional
supplement. Do not change the core postulates, restart larger lattice searches,
or advertise primes as the missing physical principle. An extension would first
need genuinely independent operational or relational inputs outside the
state-preserving symmetry orbit, together with a justified error criterion.

**Disposition:** appropriate for circulation as a clearly delimited internal
study of a failed minimal route. No outstanding presentation blocker was found
after the stated qualifications. The hypothesis is still not a predictive
cosmological theory and has not acquired a physical selector.
