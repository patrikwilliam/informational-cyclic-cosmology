## Mathematical and Physical Boundaries

- Section 5.4 fixes the same local dimension and multiplicity, ordinary trace,
  a fixed global density matrix and the entire unit-ball operator-norm distance.
  The full proof supplies the conditional-expectation normalization, trace
  adjoint, entropy-monotonicity direction and capped continuity modulus.
- The growing-dimension sequence prevents an unqualified dimension-free or
  continuum conclusion. Sampled observables do not certify the required metric.
- Restricted-state entropy, POVM entropy and cosmological entropy remain
  separate. The finite proof requires neither purity nor a stationary state;
  it does not derive physical access or a selector.
- The transport example fixes a supplied path and stopping point. Co-transport
  preserves entropy, and a returned concrete factor at fixed state has the
  same endpoint entropy. The path generator is not automatically a stationary
  global Hamiltonian. None is presented as a new no-go result for ICC.
- Mass inaccessibility alone does not establish conformal invariance.
  Gravitational comparisons require shared calibration and horizon accounting.
- The author, ORCID, all-versions DOI, license and earlier selector conclusions
  are retained. No new version-specific DOI or publication status is invented.
